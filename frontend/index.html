How buying works

Three steps, no branch visits, no card details typed into our site.

Step 1

Create an account

Register with your name, email and a password so we can track your order and delivery.

Step 2

Pick your iPhone

Compare models, storage and price, and add the one you want to your order.

Step 3

Pay via your bank

You’re redirected to Yoco’s secure payment page to complete your purchase safely. We never see your card details.

Why pay this way

Your bank details are entered on your bank's own site, not ours.

Nothing stored here

Just Cell It never sees or stores your card number, banking password, or OTPs. Payment approval happens entirely on Yoco's secure platform.

Instant confirmation

Yoco confirms your payment as soon as it’s approved, so your order is marked paid automatically.

Backed by your bank's security

You're protected by the same login and fraud checks your bank already uses for its own app.
